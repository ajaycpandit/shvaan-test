Our story

Started with a simple belief: dogs deserve more than kennels. With over a decade of hands-on experience in pet care, we created Shvaan Pet Care to offer the personal attention and safe environment every dog deserves when their family is away.

We believe your dog should feel safe, loved, and entertained every moment they're with us. We focus on reducing anxiety, encouraging play and socialization, and keeping you updated with daily photos and updates.

Our space

Our Austin home includes climate-controlled indoor play areas, cozy sleeping spaces, and a secure outdoor yard for playtime and enrichment. We maintain strict cleanliness standards and ensure constant supervision of all dogs.

We limit capacity to ensure every dog gets the individualized attention they deserve—never a crowded facility.

Our promise

How we care for your dog

🛡️ Safety first

All dogs are supervised at all times, in secure spaces with proper fencing. We require current vaccinations (Rabies, DHPP, Bordetella), and we monitor each dog's health and behavior throughout their stay.

❤️ Genuine attention

One-on-one playtime, training reinforcement, cuddles, and nap time. We know every dog's personality and adjust care to their needs.

📸 Stay in the loop

Daily photos and updates shared via email, so you can see your pup playing, napping, and enjoying their stay.

🎓 Experience

10+ years of hands-on dog care experience. Trained in pet first aid, behavior management, and health awareness.
